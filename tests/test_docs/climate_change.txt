Climate Change Report 2023
Global Temperature Rise
The average global temperature has increased by approximately 1.1 degrees Celsius compared to pre-industrial levels. The Paris Agreement aims to limit this increase to 1.5 degrees Celsius by reducing greenhouse gas emissions.

Carbon Dioxide Emissions
Global CO2 emissions reached 36.8 billion tonnes in 2023. China is the largest emitter at 11.5 billion tonnes, followed by the United States at 5.2 billion tonnes, and India at 2.7 billion tonnes.

Sea Level Rise
Global sea levels have risen by 20 centimeters since 1900. The rate of rise is accelerating, currently at 4.5 millimeters per year. Coastal cities including Mumbai, Shanghai, and Miami face significant flooding risks.

Renewable Energy
Solar panel efficiency has improved from 15% in 2010 to 23% in 2023. Wind energy capacity reached 837 GW globally in 2023. Germany generates 46% of its electricity from renewable sources.

Electric Vehicles
Electric vehicle sales reached 14 million units in 2023, representing 18% of all new car sales. Tesla holds 20% market share in the EV sector. Battery costs have decreased by 89% since 2010.

Deforestation
The Amazon rainforest lost 10,000 square kilometers in 2023. Indonesia reduced its deforestation rate by 30% between 2019 and 2023. Forests absorb approximately 30% of human CO2 emissions annually.

Ocean Acidification
Ocean pH levels have dropped from 8.2 to 8.1 since the Industrial Revolution. Coral reefs are experiencing bleaching events at unprecedented rates. The Great Barrier Reef has lost 50% of its coral since 1995.

Ice Cap Melting
Arctic sea ice is declining at a rate of 13% per decade. Antarctica lost 2.7 trillion tonnes of ice between 1992 and 2020. Greenland's ice sheet is melting six times faster than in the 1980s.

Climate Refugees
Approximately 20 million people are displaced annually due to climate-related disasters. By 2050, projections estimate 216 million climate migrants. Bangladesh is the most climate-vulnerable country, with 17% of its population at risk.

Paris Agreement Targets
195 countries signed the Paris Agreement in 2015. The agreement targets net-zero emissions by 2050. Current policies project a 2.7 degree Celsius increase by 2100, exceeding the agreed target.
